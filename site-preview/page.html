How it plays

Your brain, split in two

Thread

Each lane gets its own wall with its own gap. Steer both orbs through at once. Skim a wall without touching it for a GRAZE.

clean passes chain a combo · ×1 more every 5

Pump

Flick a thumb up and down to fill the tank. Pump both thumbs in rhythm and it fills fastest.

both thumbs together · 1.8× charge

Wreck

At full, the tank lights up. You run faster, coins pull toward you, and slate shatters on contact. Chain enough smashes and the whole field goes.

boost 1.85× speed · 4–6 smashes · WRECKED
Country vs country

Who hits the wall first?

Put a flag on each orb and every run becomes a match. Brazil on the left, Argentina on the right: the side that touches a wall loses, and the comments pick the next matchup.

Controls

Built for two thumbs

PhoneLeft thumb drives the left orb, right thumb the right. Put both down to start. Pump up and down to charge.
ComputerOne lane, the orb follows your mouse. Click to start and to run again. The full game wants a phone.
