How to play

Left thumb drives the left lane, right thumb the right. Thread the gaps, take the coins. Lift a thumb and that side stops moving — it does not stop being hit.

Pump both thumbs up and down to fill the tank. At 100 it ignites and drains as you ride it. Faster, coins pull to you, and barriers break on you instead of the other way round. Every smash refills the tank, so you can stay lit and wreck as long as you keep hitting things.
