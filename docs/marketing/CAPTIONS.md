# Caption and hashtag formula (researched 2026-09-25)

## The rules that changed

- **Instagram caps hashtags at 5** per post or Reel (since December 2025).
  More gets blocked or trimmed, and posts over the cap lose Explore and
  Reels distribution. Hashtags in the first comment count toward the 5.
- **Captions are now search keywords.** Instagram indexes caption text and
  weighs the first 125 characters (what shows before "more") most. TikTok
  indexes the caption, the on-screen text and the spoken audio together.
- **TikTok: 3–5 specific hashtags** beat many generic ones; mixed tags send
  the algorithm mixed signals.
- **YouTube Shorts:** title up to 100 characters, key words in the first 50,
  no hashtags in the title. Put 3–5 hashtags at the end of the description;
  the first 3 show above the title. #16 onward is ignored.
- **Everywhere:** no #fyp, #viral or #trending. They say nothing about the video.

## The formula

```
LINE 1  Hook + main keyword, under ~100 characters.
        Same words as the on-screen text.
LINE 2  One sentence of context with a second keyword.
LINE 3  One action, the same one the end card asks for.
TAGS    3–5 total: 1 category + 1–2 niche + 1 about this video + 1 brand.
```

**Keywords to rotate** (use one or two per caption, in plain sentences):
two-thumb game, mobile game, brain game, indie game, country vs country,
hand-eye coordination, iPhone game, Android game.

**Hashtag bank**

| Slot | Pick one |
|---|---|
| Category | #mobilegame #mobilegaming #indiegame |
| Niche | #twothumbs #braingame #gamedev #indiedev #comingsoon (launch posts only) |
| This video | #brazilvsargentina style (the matchup), #rivalry, the sport (#football #rugby #cricket #hockey) |
| Brand | #belltheory |

**Per platform**

| | Instagram Reel / post | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line 1 | Under 125 chars, keyword in it | Hook, keyword early | Becomes the title (≤100, keyword in first 50) |
| Hashtags | Max 5, in the caption | 3–5 | 3–5 at the end of the description |
| Extra | — | Say the keyword out loud if there's voice | Description can repeat lines 2–3 |

**Worked shape** (a rivalry round, not a final caption):

```
Brazil vs Argentina, but it's a two-thumb brain game 🇧🇷🇦🇷
Two AI orbs, one wall. First to hit it loses.
Who should've won? Comment below 👇
#mobilegame #twothumbs #brazilvsargentina #football #belltheory
```

## Launch pair (post + reel)

Post the Reel first; the static post the next day, or as a carousel cover.
Pin both to the profile until launch day.
