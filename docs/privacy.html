Bell TheoryPrivacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · applies to the Bell Theory app for iOS and Android, bundle id com.belltheory.game

Bell Theory collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party code. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere. What the game remembers, it remembers on your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no way to identify you and does not try to. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or any other personal information, and it does not generate an identifier of its own.

2. Accounts, ads, analytics, third parties

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud save.
  • No ads. The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising software.
  • No analytics. We do not measure how you play, how long you play, or whether you play. No crash reports are sent.
  • No third-party SDKs. The app contains no code from analytics, advertising, attribution, or social companies. The only code in it is the game and the open-source shell it runs in.

3. What stays on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data so it behaves the way you left it. All of it is stored in the app's own storage on your phone and is never transmitted.

DataWhat it is
SettingsSound on or off, volume, impact, trail length, glow, follow speed, lane grid, spectrum bars, haptic buzz, play mode, and the colour of each orb.
Best distanceYour highest score, kept so the death screen can show it.
A one-time hint flagWhether the pump hint has been shown, so it is shown once.
Orb picture (optional)If you choose a photo for an orb, the app makes a small square copy of it (160 pixels) on the device and stores that copy with your settings. The original is not touched and the copy is never uploaded. You can replace or remove it in Customize.
Purchase stateWhether the one-time unlock has been bought, as reported to the app by the App Store or Google Play.

Deleting it: uninstall the app. Every item above is removed with it. The reset settings button in Customize clears the settings but keeps your best distance, since that is earned rather than configured.

4. Network

The app makes no network requests during play. It does not need an internet connection and works the same with Wi-Fi and mobile data turned off.

The only time your phone talks to anyone on the app's behalf is the optional one-time purchase, and that conversation is between your phone and Apple or Google, not us:

  • On iOS the purchase is handled by the App Store (StoreKit) under Apple's privacy policy.
  • On Android it is handled by Google Play Billing under Google's privacy policy.

The app receives a yes-or-no purchase state from the store. It receives no payment details and no personal information, and we do not operate a server that the app reports to.

5. Permissions

  • Photos (only if you choose a picture for an orb): the app opens the system picker and reads only the picture you select. It never scans your library.
  • Haptics: used for the buzz on impacts. No data is involved and it can be turned off in Customize.
  • The app does not request location, camera access beyond the optional picture, microphone, contacts, notifications, or Bluetooth.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to review, correct, or delete beyond uninstalling the app.

7. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to access, export, correct, or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control. If you believe this policy is wrong about something, write to us at the address below and we will look into it.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released, the effective date at the top will change, and the app's store listing will be updated to match. A change that reduces what the app remembers, or that only reorganises this text, may be made without notice.
