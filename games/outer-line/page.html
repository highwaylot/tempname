OUTER LINE

Walls are floors.

A tunnel runner on a space metro. Run the line stop to stop, jump the gaps, and step onto a wall when the floor runs out: the tunnel turns and the wall becomes the floor.

  • Free in your browser
  • Keyboard or touch
  • No account
How it rides

Ten stops, three sections of track

Ride

The train doesn't stop and neither do you. Steer left and right to stay on the tiles.

speed 8 → 12.5 across the line

Turn

Out of floor? Step onto the wall. The tunnel turns so the wall is under your feet.

6- and 8-sided tunnels

Jump

Some stretches have no track at all. Jump them; you can steer in the air.

One route a day

Same route for everyone. Post your card.

Every day at midnight UTC the line lays a new route. Everyone in the world rides the same one. Your first ride of the day goes on your card; practice as much as you like after.

  • No streaks. Missing a day costs nothing.
  • No accounts, no leaderboard: the card says it.
  • The card never shows the route, so it can't spoil it.
